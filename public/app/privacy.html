Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how Meridian Protocol handles information when you use the current website or Android app. It describes the current account-security service and public links; future wallet or transaction features will require updated disclosures before activation.

1. Operator and contact

Service name: Meridian Protocol
Launch country: Pakistan
Privacy and support contact: admin@meridianprotocol.site

2. Information processed

  • Account and authentication: email address, password submitted to the configured authentication provider, verification status, sessions, and authentication factors you choose to enable, such as passkeys or TOTP.
  • Profile and consent: username, display name, language and timezone preferences, an optional profile photo you choose to upload, the versions of the Terms and Privacy Policy accepted, and consent timestamps. Photos are resized on your device and kept in a private account folder.
  • Technical and security data: authentication and infrastructure providers may process IP address, device/browser information, request metadata, and security events to deliver and protect their services. The exact fields depend on provider configuration.
  • Support correspondence: if you email us, we receive the information and attachments you choose to send. The app's contact form opens your email app; it does not transmit or store the draft itself.

3. Current blockchain and third-party links

The current app links to public blockchain explorers and community sites but does not connect a wallet, request a wallet address, read wallet balances, or submit blockchain transactions. If you open an external site, that provider may collect data under its own policy. Public blockchain records are visible independently of Meridian and may be permanent. This app does not receive a copy merely because it displays an explorer link.

4. Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, maintain profiles and consent records, provide account controls, respond to support requests, maintain the service, and meet applicable legal obligations. This app does not request precise GPS location or continuously track your location. The current frontend includes no advertising or analytics integration.

5. Service providers and data location

Supabase is the configured authentication and database provider. Read-only token supply requests are sent to the public BNB Chain RPC endpoint; opening BscScan or a community channel sends your browser to that third party. Provider processing, subprocessors, hosting location, and retention depend on the project and provider settings chosen by the operator. Optional services such as Cloudflare Turnstile and Google sign-in process information only when configured and used. Review each provider's current privacy terms for its own processing practices.

6. Storage, security, and retention

Passwords are sent to the authentication provider for verification and are not stored as plaintext in Meridian's profile table. The Android app uses a Keystore-backed storage integration for its authentication session. Web sessions use tab-scoped browser storage that JavaScript running on the page can access. Database profile access is protected by row-level security. We use these controls to reduce risk, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Profile data and an optional profile photo are retained while an account is active. Deleting an account removes the profile and its photo when the configured account-deletion service is available; provider logs or protected backups may retain data for provider-defined periods. Account deletion can be requested in the app if the deletion service is available, or by emailing support. A request cannot remove public blockchain records or data retained independently by third parties. The operator reviews requests and handles them subject to applicable law and technical capabilities.

7. Your choices and requests

You may update available profile information in the app, sign out or manage available authentication factors, and contact admin@meridianprotocol.site to ask about access, correction, or deletion of account information. Do not include a password, verification code, recovery code, seed phrase, or private key in a request.

8. Children

The service is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly invite children to create accounts. Contact the operator if you believe a child has submitted personal information so the request can be reviewed.

9. Policy changes

We may update this Policy when the service or provider configuration changes. Material changes receive a new version number and are presented for renewed acceptance where required. The version currently in effect is shown at the top of this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email admin@meridianprotocol.site.
